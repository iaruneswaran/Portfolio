03 // ABOUT ME

Hello, I’m Arun Eswaran.

UI/UX designer and researcher collaborating with early-stage teams to explore and shape early product ideas. I specialize in research-driven UX for community platforms and AI-assisted experiences, grounding design decisions in real user behavior. Alongside this, I study how people interact with AI for emotional and reflective purposes, including co-authoring a research paper on emotionally driven use of conversational AI. I also explore AI-supported and prompt-driven product development workflows, using rapid prototyping approaches to quickly translate ideas into testable experiences. I’m most interested in work where research and UX intersect to build thoughtful, blending user needs with business goals.
